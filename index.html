About the authorthe writer

Hello, I'm Ishaan.

I've been writing for brands since 2021: SEO long-form for SaaS companies, test pages for a diagnostics platform, guides for students moving abroad, web copy, football columns and, lately, Hinglish scripts for Instagram reels about Delhi's markets, temples and food.

I started in equity research, which left me with one habit I've kept: find the story inside the numbers before writing a word. Then I write it the way a person would say it.

I live in Noida, read too much about old Delhi, and will happily talk fragrances with anyone who asks.
